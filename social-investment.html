Social investment / Our community

Stronger roots.
Greater possibilities.

Makarapa Day is a celebration of our people, on and off the stage. Our community initiatives bring together outdoor experiences, local enterprise and youth sport in Sekhukhune.

01 / Community & connection

Step by step.
We rise together.

The Makarapa Day Hike is about more than reaching the top. It is a shared experience that brings people into nature, encourages teamwork and creates a moment to reconnect.

Every step is a reminder of what we can do together: support each other, embrace a challenge and build a stronger sense of community.

02 / Local enterprise

Local business.
Room to grow.

The Makarapa Day Business Expo gives entrepreneurs, startups and SMMEs a platform to showcase their products and connect with potential customers and partners.

By creating visibility for local ventures, the initiative supports the township economy and the people shaping its future.

03 / Youth & sport

More than a game.
A chance to shine.

The Makarapa Day Top 8 Soccer Tournament brings regional football clubs together to celebrate local talent, discipline and teamwork.

Hosted at Lion Brothers Sports Ground in Ga-Nchabeleng, the initiative creates a competitive platform for young players and a shared occasion for the community around them.
